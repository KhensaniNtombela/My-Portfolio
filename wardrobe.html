AI Wardrobe Stylist

A group-built AI fashion styling assistant designed to explore how generative AI and prompt engineering can make everyday outfit planning more personalised, convenient and engaging.

Explore Gemini Project Back to Portfolio

The Problem

Choosing what to wear can take time, especially when people want to create outfits from clothes they already own. Finding inspiration and keeping track of outfit ideas can also be inconvenient.

This project explores how an AI-powered styling assistant could help make those everyday decisions easier.

Mission and Purpose

The mission is to explore a more accessible and personalised approach to outfit planning through generative AI.

The project aims to connect fashion inspiration with practical wardrobe organisation in one user-focused experience.

Vision

To explore a future where people can make more confident styling choices, get inspiration from their existing wardrobe and enjoy a more personalised fashion experience.

The Solution

The concept combines an AI fashion stylist with digital wardrobe organisation. It explores how prompt engineering can guide AI to provide personalised styling assistance.

The project was developed collaboratively, with generative AI supporting the creation of the application concept.

Key Features

The project concept brings together the following features, as described in the portfolio:

  • AI-assisted fashion styling
  • Digital wardrobe organisation
  • Outfit saving and inspiration
  • Chat history
  • Wear tracking

Tools and Technologies

The project explores generative AI and prompt engineering to shape the fashion assistant experience.

Learning and Future Improvements

This project provided an opportunity to explore how prompts can communicate product requirements and guide an AI-assisted application concept.

Potential future improvements include refining the styling recommendations, improving wardrobe organisation, and testing the experience with users. These are opportunities for further development, not claims about existing functionality.
